Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy & Cookie Policy explains how personal data may be processed when you visit the LocalKeyRemapper website available at https://localkeyremapper.github.io.

This Policy primarily applies to the website. The LocalKeyRemapper macOS application is described separately below because its privacy characteristics differ from those of the website.

2. LocalKeyRemapper macOS application

The LocalKeyRemapper macOS application operates locally on your Mac.

The application does not contain advertising, analytics or telemetry and does not transmit your remapping profiles, rules or application settings to LocalKeyRemapper servers.

Advertising technologies, cookies and consent mechanisms described in this Policy concern the LocalKeyRemapper website and are not embedded in the LocalKeyRemapper macOS application.

3. Website operation and data protection responsibility

LocalKeyRemapper is an open-source project and is not operated through a separately incorporated company or other legal entity.

The website is maintained through the LocalKeyRemapper open-source project and its GitHub organization.

For processing activities relating to this website, responsibility under applicable data protection law depends on the person or persons who determine the purposes and means of the relevant processing activity.

Where such decisions are made jointly by multiple project maintainers or administrators, those persons may act jointly to the extent provided by applicable data protection law.

Third-party service providers such as Google and GitHub may independently determine the purposes and means of certain processing activities carried out through their services. Their respective privacy notices and data protection terms apply to those activities.

Questions concerning this website or this Privacy & Cookie Policy may be submitted through the LocalKeyRemapper website repository on GitHub:

https://github.com/LocalKeyRemapper/LocalKeyRemapper.github.io

Because GitHub repositories and issues may be publicly accessible, users should not include sensitive personal data, confidential information, identification documents, passwords or other information that they do not wish to make public.

4. Categories of personal data that may be processed

4.1 Hosting and technical data

When you access the website, technical information necessary for the operation, delivery and security of the website may be processed.

This information may include:

  • IP address;
  • date and time of the request;
  • requested page or resource;
  • browser and device information;
  • operating system information;
  • referring page or URL;
  • network and request information; and
  • other technical information necessary to deliver or secure the website.

4.2 Advertising data

Where Google AdSense or approved advertising technologies are used, information relating to advertising delivery and measurement may be processed.

Depending on the user's location, privacy choices, advertising configuration and applicable law, this information may include:

  • IP address;
  • browser and device information;
  • approximate location derived from technical information;
  • page URL and page context;
  • cookies and similar identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • consent and privacy signals;
  • advertisement impressions;
  • advertisement interactions;
  • fraud-prevention and security signals; and
  • other information used for advertising purposes as permitted by applicable law.

4.3 Information voluntarily provided by users

If you contact the project or interact with its public GitHub repositories, information contained in your communication may be processed in order to review or respond to it.

Information submitted through public GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions or other public GitHub features may be visible to anyone.

5. Purposes of processing

Personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

  • delivering and displaying the website;
  • maintaining the technical operation of the website;
  • protecting the website and its infrastructure against abuse, fraud or security threats;
  • displaying advertisements;
  • selecting advertisements where permitted;
  • measuring advertisement delivery and performance where permitted;
  • preventing invalid advertising traffic, abuse and fraud;
  • recording and respecting privacy and consent choices;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations; and
  • responding to communications concerning the project or this Policy.

6. Legal bases under European data protection law

Where the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") applies, processing may rely on different legal bases depending on the specific activity.

6.1 Consent

Where required by applicable law, consent is requested before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies or before processing personal data for personalized advertising and related purposes.

Where processing is based on consent, the applicable legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

6.2 Legitimate interests

Certain processing necessary for operating, maintaining, securing and protecting the website may be based on legitimate interests pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, where those interests are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of the user.

Such interests may include website security, fraud prevention, protection against abuse and maintaining the technical availability of the service.

6.3 Legal obligations

Personal data may also be processed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation pursuant to Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

The use of legitimate interests or another legal basis does not replace consent where consent is specifically required under applicable laws governing cookies, device storage or advertising technologies.

This website may use Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google and other advertising technology providers may process information in connection with the delivery, selection, measurement, security and personalization of advertisements, depending on applicable law and the user's privacy choices.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites where legally permitted and where the necessary consent has been obtained.

Google's use of advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet, subject to applicable privacy requirements and the user's choices.

Depending on the circumstances, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized or otherwise subject to restricted data processing.

7.1 Personalized advertising

Personalized advertising may use information concerning previous browsing activity, interests, advertising interactions or other information in order to select advertisements considered more relevant to a particular user.

Where required by law, personalized advertising is only enabled after the required consent has been obtained.

7.2 Non-personalized or limited advertising

Non-personalized advertisements are not selected based on a user's previous browsing behaviour for advertising personalization.

However, non-personalized or limited advertising may still require the processing of technical information, such as IP addresses, browser information, page context and security signals.

Certain advertising operations may also use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as basic ad delivery, frequency management, aggregated measurement, security, fraud prevention or detection of invalid traffic.

Where consent is legally required for such technologies, they are subject to the user's consent choices.

Where required, this website uses a consent management platform ("CMP") to provide information about advertising technologies and allow users to make privacy choices.

The CMP may allow users to:

  • accept relevant processing;
  • reject relevant processing;
  • manage individual purposes or advertising providers where available;
  • review their privacy choices; and

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other jurisdictions where applicable, consent information may be communicated to participating advertising providers through applicable industry-standard consent and privacy frameworks.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

Rejecting optional advertising processing does not prevent access to the principal content of this website, although it may affect the type of advertising displayed and the advertising functions that are available.
